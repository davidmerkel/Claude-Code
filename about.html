Background

I grew up fascinated by how systems — social, economic, and technological — shape behavior at scale. That curiosity led me to study psychology at Amherst College, where I graduated magna cum laude with distinction and captained the varsity squash team, before spending several years at the intersection of persuasion, data, and strategy.

After Amherst I joined People First (formerly Main Street One), an online persuasion startup that grew from a handful of people into a 60+ person profitable company during my time there. I worked across every function — from analysis to senior strategy to Chief of Staff — running multi-channel campaigns in English and Spanish for brands, political campaigns, unions, and NGOs.

I then moved into the startup and venture world. As a Venture Fellow at Third Sphere, I was seconded to Reynko, a company revolutionizing computational fluid dynamics for industrial applications. I quickly became their Head of Business Development, building their Early Access Program, standing up CRM operations, and opening doors with Fortune 500 companies in manufacturing, automotive, and aerospace.

In between, I spent a summer as a Graduate Intern on the White House Industrial Innovation Team, working on net-zero frameworks and industrial strategy across agencies.

Now at MIT Sloan and Harvard Kennedy School, I'm studying entrepreneurial finance, venture capital, tech competition policy, and the governance of emerging technologies. I'm particularly interested in AI, energy transition, and what it takes to build enduring companies.

Outside of work I play squash and poker, hike, bike, and speak fluent Spanish.
