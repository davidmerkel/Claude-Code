Our Story

Built on trust, driven by results

We're a team of strategists, engineers, and designers united by a single goal: helping our clients succeed.

Since 2013

From a small consultancy to a full-service partner

Apex Solutions was founded in 2013 by a trio of management consultants who believed that small and mid-sized businesses deserved the same calibre of strategic support as Fortune 500 companies — without the bloated fees.

Over twelve years we've expanded our team to 40+ professionals across strategy, engineering, design, and marketing. We've served 500+ clients in 30+ countries, but our values haven't changed a bit.

Today we're proud to be the long-term partner of choice for growth-stage companies who want to move fast without breaking things.
